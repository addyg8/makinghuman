# About

*makinghuman* is a collection of essays exploring the intersection of science, nature, indigenous knowledge, and what it means to live as a human being in this world.

The site is written and maintained by someone who spends their days at a lab bench, pipetting invisible liquid into invisible liquid, and wondering how science got so small — and how it might grow large again.
